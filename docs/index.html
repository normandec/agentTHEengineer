How it works

A council that shows its work

  1. Research — independent researchers collect evidence and counter-evidence.
  2. Draft & challenge — an explainer drafts, while a critic, verifier and calculator check it.
  3. Revise & vote — the answer is revised; online council members vote. Concerns remain visible.
  4. Teach visually — an illustrator adds a labelled sketch on this shared sheet.

This process reduces errors; it cannot eliminate them. Check important claims against the linked sources.
